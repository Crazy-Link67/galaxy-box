## 📖 Overview

**GALAXYBOX** is a 2D god sandbox simulator inspired by classic pixel world simulators. As an all-powerful deity, you command nature, unleash apocalypse upon civilizations, build thriving worlds from scratch, possess and pilot massive monsters and combat vehicles, and perform ancient cosmic rituals across a persistent cellular matrix.

Everything runs in real time at **60 FPS** with **zero build steps or external dependencies**—simply launch `index.html` in any web browser!

## ⚡ Core Categories & Powers

GALAXYBOX organizes its vast arsenal of god powers into **6 primary categories**:

### 1. 💥 Destruction & Chaos
* **☢️ Atomic Nuke**: Radioactive fireball creating deep fallout craters, incinerating matter, and vaporizing creatures.
* **🌌 Antimatter Bomb**: Implosion singularity deleting all terrain and physical entities in range.
* **🕳️ Black Hole**: Roaming gravitational singularity sucking in terrain, fluids, and living creatures.
* **⚡ Orbital Laser**: Continuous divine death ray carving molten trenches across the earth.
* **☄️ Meteor Strike**: Flaming extraterrestrial impact that blasts massive craters and sets fires.
* **🚀 Cluster Missiles**: Multi-stage projectile salvo triggering cascading micro-detonations.
* **👆 Tsar Finger (Disintegrator)**: Divine touch that instantly dissolves individual pixels and blocks.
* **🧨 TNT Explosive**: Industrial explosive detonating in fiery concussive shockwaves.
* **⭐ Supernova**: Blinding cosmic star explosion engulfing entire regions in stellar heat.
* **🧪 Corrosion Bomb**: Shower of high-potency toxic acid dissolving solid matter.

### 2. 🌪️ Nature & Disasters
* **🌋 Volcano**: Persistent geologic feature spewing endless smoke, ash clouds, and molten lava.
* **🌪️ Tornado**: Destructive roaming twister lifting trees, debris, and creatures into the air.
* **⚡ Earthquake**: Tectonic fissure ripping jagged chasms through terrain.
* **🌩️ Lightning Strike**: High-voltage electrical strike that ignites trees, harms enemies, or empowers special creatures.
* **🌊 Tsunami Deluge**: Massive tidal wave that floods coastlines and inland territories.
* **🌧️ Reviving Rain**: Quenches wildfires, hydrates dry soil, and replenishes fertile flora.
* **❄️ Blizzard**: Frigid winter storm freezing water into solid ice and blanketing the earth in snow.
* **☣️ Acid Storm**: Corrosive toxic rain dissolving forests and poisoning civilizations.
* **🔥 Wildfire**: Fast-spreading wildfire that sweeps through grasslands and dense canopies.

### 3. 🗺️ Landscaping & Terraforming
* **Terrain Types**: Deep Ocean, Shallow Water, Beach Sand, Fertile Soil, Grassland, Dense Forest, Mountain Stone, High Peak, Snow, Solid Ice, Molten Lava, Acid Sludge, and Indestructible Bedrock.
* **Cosmic Tiles**:
  * **🌌 Cosmic Nebula**: Shimmering interstellar fluid nebula.
  * **✨ Stardust Land**: Luminous celestial stardust soil.
* **Terraforming Tools**:
  * **⬆️ Shovel (Raise)**: Elevates terrain to form hills and mountain peaks.
  * **⬇️ Shovel (Lower)**: Carves valleys, lakes, and deep ocean trenches.
  * **🧽 Sponge Drain**: Absorbs and clears water, lava, and acid without modifying ground tiles.
  * **🌾 Life Sprout (Fertilizer)**: Seeds lush flora, trees, flowers, and agriculture.

### 4. ✨ Various Powers & Miracles
* **✋ God's Hand**: Grab any living creature, drag it anywhere across the world, and fling it with momentum!
* **☀️ Heat Ray & ❄️ Freeze Ray**: Directional thermal and cryogenic beams to melt or freeze target areas.
* **✨ Divine Blessing**: Bestows golden holy auras, doubled HP, super speed, and immortality.
* **💀 Void Curse**: Weakens targets, halving their maximum HP and causing permanent damage.
* **🧟 Zombie Plague**: Contagious biological pathogen turning villagers into infectious undead.
* **🪙 Coin of Fate (The Snap)**: Flips the cosmic coin to dissolve exactly 50% of all living beings on the map into dust.
* **💚 Divine Heal**: Restores all injured creatures back to peak vitality.
* **🩸 Bloodlust**: Forces nearby creatures into a violent frenzy where they attack friends and foes alike.
* **📈 Titan Ray & 📉 Shrink Ray**: Dynamically enlarges or shrinks creatures to gigantic or miniature scales.
* **🛸 Alien Saucer**: Spawns an extraterrestrial UFO with tractor beams abducting cows and humans.
* **🔍 Miracle Eye (Inspector)**: Detailed inspector HUD displaying species stats, health, attack power, kingdom allegiances, and active traits.

### 5. 👥 Creatures, Civilizations, Titans & Vehicles
* **Four Intelligent Civilizations**:
  * **🧑 Humans**: Balanced settlers who construct houses, establish villages, and expand kingdom borders.
  * **🧝 Elves**: Agile woodland inhabitants with archery skills and deep connections to nature.
  * **👹 Orcs**: Brutal, hearty warriors with high health and aggressive expansionism.
  * **🧔 Dwarves**: Master miners and fortress builders who thrive in rocky and mountainous terrain.
* **Animals & Monsters**:
  * Sheep, Cows, Grey Wolves, Grizzly Bears, Fire Dragons, Rock Golems, Zombies, Skeletons, Nether Demons, and Alien Scouts.
* **Colossal Titans & Mythic Beasts**:
  * **🦀 Crabzilla**: Giant armored titan crab with twin eye laser rays and mega seismic stomps.
  * **🦖 Kaiju Godzilla**: Radioactive apex predator with glowing atomic dorsal spines and molten atomic breath.
  * **🦅 Solar Phoenix**: Immortal avian entity firing solar flare fireballs and exploding into flames upon death.
  * **🦑 Abyssal Kraken**: Oceanic leviathan gliding through deep water with tentacle slams.
  * **🐍 Venom Hydra**: Multi-headed regenerative serpent spitting streams of corrosive venom.
  * **🧊 Frost Titan**: Glacial behemoth freezing terrain and casting explosive Frost Novas.
* **🚜 Controllable Vehicles**:
  * **🚜 Battle Tank**: Armored combat vehicle with rotating cannon turret and dual treads.
  * **🚢 Battleship**: Heavy naval warship with twin artillery turrets and water wake dynamics.
  * **🚁 Attack Chopper**: Aerial gunship with animated spinning rotors, vulcan machine guns, and rocket salvos.
  * **🚀 Cosmic Starfighter**: Futuristic interstellar fighter with twin photon lasers and warp shockwave thrusters.
* **✨ Galaxy Guardian**: Celestial cosmic guardian radiating planetary rings and starlight lasers.

### 6. ⚙️ Menu & World Systems
* **🌍 World Generator**: Procedural world generator with custom seed support and built-in templates:
  * *Continents, Archipelago, Pangea, Ring of Fire, Chaos World, Flat Grass, Blank Ocean*, and the secret *Spiral Galaxy* template.
* **💾 World Save & Load**: 5 local persistence slots and full JSON import/export functionality.
* **⚙️ Settings**: Audio master volume slider, mute toggle, and live simulation performance telemetry.
* **📖 God's Codex**: Comprehensive in-game manual and lore guide.
* **🗑️ Reset World**: Instantly wipe and reset the sandbox canvas.

## 🎮 Direct Creature Control ("Possess Mode")

Take direct control of **any creature, monster, or vehicle** in the world:

1. Click the **"🎮 Possess / Control"** tool in Category 5 and click on any creature or vehicle.
2. Alternatively, hover your cursor over any entity and press the **`C`** key.
3. Spawning **Crabzilla**, **Kaiju**, or any **Vehicle** automatically puts you in the pilot's seat!

### Controls While Possessing:
| Key / Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`W` `A` `S` `D`** / **Arrows** | Move entity in real-time |
| **Left-Click** | Primary Attack (Eye lasers, cannon shells, fireballs, vulcan guns) |
| **Right-Click** / **`Q`** / **`E`** | Special Ability (Seismic stomp, rocket salvo, warp boost, nuclear roar) |
| **`Escape`** | Exit control and return to God View |

## 🌌 The Great Galaxy Sacrifice Ritual

Perform the ancient cosmic ritual to permanently unlock the secret **Spiral Galaxy** world generator template:

1. Open the **Creatures tab (5)** and spawn a **Galaxy Guardian (`✨`)**.
2. Switch to **Destruction & Chaos (1)** and select the **Atomic Nuke (`☢️`)**.
3. Drop the Atomic Nuke **directly on top of the Galaxy Guardian**!
4. **The Supernova**: The guardian's core implodes into a cosmic supernova, transforming surrounding terrain into **Cosmic Nebulae** and **Stardust Land**.
5. **The Unlock**: The celebration banner appears, and the secret **Spiral Galaxy** map template is permanently unlocked in the World Generator!

## 🎨 Creature Creator Studio

Open the **"🎨 Creature Creator"** tool in Category 5 to build custom entities:
* **Custom Name & Color**: Name your creation and pick its palette.
* **Archetype Base**: Choose from Crabzilla, Kaiju, Flying Dragon, Frost Titan, Human Hero, or Rock Golem.
* **Dynamic Sliders**: Configure Health (up to 10,000 HP), Attack (up to 300), Speed, and Scale (up to 5.0x).
* **Combinable Traits**:
  * *Titan, Laser Eyes, Fireproof, Water Walker, Regeneration, Super Speed, Immortal, Venomous, Explosive Death, Electrocharged, Bloodthirsty, Peaceful.*
* Click **"🌟 Spawn & Control"** to immediately bring your creature to life and pilot it!

## 🛠️ Project Structure

```
galaxy-box/
├── index.html          # Main HTML5 UI markup, canvases, HUD docks, and modals
├── style.css           # Retro pixel aesthetic, glassmorphism HUD styles, and animations
├── README.md           # Game documentation and manual
└── js/
    ├── audio.js        # Procedural Web Audio API SFX synthesizer (zero audio files needed)
    ├── particles.js    # Zero-allocation particle pool engine for explosions, smoke, spells
    ├── world.js        # Cellular automata tile grid, terrain generation, and fluid physics
    ├── entities.js     # Entity ECS, species stats, AI behaviors, kingdoms, traits, projectiles
    ├── disasters.js    # Natural and apocalyptic disasters, explosions, black holes, weather
    ├── renderer.js     # Crisp 2D canvas pixel renderer, animated sprites, camera, minimap
    ├── ui.js           # Category menus, tool palettes, inspector, creator studio, modals
    └── game.js         # Core game loop, input dispatcher, possession controller, save engine
```

## 🚀 Getting Started

No installation, build steps, or server required!

1. Clone or download the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/your-username/galaxy-box.git
   ```
2. Double-click **`index.html`** or run:
   ```bash
   Start-Process index.html
   ```
3. Play immediately in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Apple Safari!
