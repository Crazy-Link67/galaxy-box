📖 God's Codex & Guide

💥 1. Destruction & Chaos

Unleash supreme god powers to pulverize lands: Atomic Nukes, Antimatter Implosions, Black Holes that crush matter, Orbital Lasers, Meteors, and Tsar Disintegration.

🌪️ 2. Nature & Natural Disasters

Harness the primal forces of the planet: Active Volcanoes that pour hot magma, wandering Tornadoes, seismic Earthquakes, Lightning bolts, Tsunamis, and Acid Storms.

🗺️ 3. Landscaping

Sculpt oceans, sands, fertile soil, grasslands, mountain peaks, lava, and acid. Use the Elevation Shovels to raise or lower land, the Sponge to drain floods, and Life Sprout to instantly bloom forests.

✨ 4. Various Powers & Miracles

Pick up creatures with God's Hand and throw them across the sea. Bestow Divine Blessings, cast dark Curses, trigger a contagious Zombie Plague, flip the Coin of Fate (Thanos snap 50%), or inspect any lifeform with Miracle Eye.

👥 5. Creatures & Civilizations

Spawn Humans, Elves, Dwarves, and Orcs. Watch them build campfires, wooden cottages, and townhalls, form named kingdoms with banners, harvest wood and stone, and wage war with neighboring nations! Also populate the world with sheep, wolves, dragons, golems, and aliens.

⚙️ 6. The Menu

Generate endless procedural worlds (Continents, Archipelagos, Ring Worlds), save your creations to 5 local save slots, export/import JSON files to share with friends, and tweak sound and simulation speeds.
